Parts of this plugin are adapted from Matt Pocock's skills, https://github.com/mattpocock/skills:

- `writing-for-agents`: the writing levers (context pointers, the two loads, information hierarchy, completion criteria, leading words, pruning).
- `brainstorm`: the design tree, frontier and round mechanics of `grilling`.
- `write-spec`: the spec template of `to-spec`.
- `write-tickets`: the tracer-bullet slicing and expand–contract rules of `to-tickets`.
- `domain-modeling`: the glossary and ADR discipline and formats of `domain-modeling`.
- `tdd`: the red-green loop and test anti-patterns of `tdd`.
- `review`, `review-standards`, `review-spec`: the two-axis review and smell baseline of `code-review`.
